Dogevideos App

A private, single-owner tool for managing one YouTube channel.

This application is a personal automation tool built and used by a single individual to manage their own YouTube channel. It is not a public service, is not offered to other users, and no one besides its owner ever signs in to it.

What it does

  • Uploads videos the owner produced to their own YouTube channel.
  • Reads the channel's own statistics (views, likes, video list) to show a dashboard.
  • Reads and, only after the owner's explicit confirmation for each one, replies to or moderates comments on the owner's own videos.

Every action that changes anything on YouTube happens only after the owner reviews it and clicks confirm inside the application - nothing runs automatically without that review, except an optional, separately opt-in scheduled-release feature the owner turns on themselves for their own planned uploads.

Who can use it

Nobody but its owner. It runs on the owner's own computer, authenticated only against the owner's own Google account and the single YouTube channel they own.

See the Privacy Policy for details on what data this application accesses and how it is handled.
