Dogevideos App

A private, single-operator automation tool for one person's own social media accounts.

Dogevideos App (also known by its internal project name, yt-ai-pipeline) is a personal automation tool, built and run by a single individual (a hobby video creator) to manage their own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not a public service, is not offered to other users, and no one besides its owner ever signs in to it.

What it does

  • Uploads short-form videos the owner produced to their own connected accounts.
  • Reads each connected account's own statistics (views, likes, video list) to show a dashboard.
  • On YouTube, reads and, only after the owner's explicit confirmation for each one, replies to or moderates comments on the owner's own videos.

Every action that publishes or changes anything on a connected account happens only after the owner reviews it and clicks confirm inside the application - nothing runs automatically without that review, except an optional, separately opt-in scheduled-release feature (YouTube only) the owner turns on themselves for their own planned uploads.

Platforms

  • YouTube - active: uploading, statistics and (with a separate, later opt-in) comments.
  • TikTok - Login Kit and Content Posting API integration built for the owner's own account; videos are uploaded as private (self-only) until the developer app is approved for public posting.
  • Instagram - integration built for the owner's own account; currently paused while access is restricted at the platform level, independent of this application.

Who can use it

Nobody but its owner. It runs on the owner's own computer, authenticated only against the owner's own accounts on each platform - never anyone else's.
